# drawbluff's prompts in English (written for drawbluff; also the
# source of decoy lies), fetched by the host page (data/prompts.de.txt:
# German, written for German speakers, not translated).
#
# Format: `[tag]` starts a structure, then one prompt per line; blank
# lines and `#` comments are skipped. A round mixes the structures (no
# structure twice among three prompts in a row, `deck` in
# game/rules.ts), decoys come from the drawing's own structure first. A
# room deals no prompt twice until it dealt them all (`PromptDeck`).
#
# Every prompt is in its display form (lowercase, letters, spaces and
# apostrophes, nothing at the end) and at most 40 characters, the
# longest lie a phone takes (LIE_SOFT), so neither form nor length is a
# tell. Drawable, family-friendly, our own; no two alike (`node
# scripts/prompts.ts --near` lists near duplicates; test/prompts.test.ts
# checks all of it).
#
# The structures:
#   odd       odd combinations: a lonely lighthouse at a party
#   idiom     idioms taken literally: raining cats and dogs
#   feeling   abstract ideas and feelings: the fear of mondays
#   moment    the moment when: the moment the ice cream falls
#   secret    the secret lives of things: what the fridge does at night
#   invented  invented jobs, sports, holidays: a puddle inspector
#   contra    misleading or contradictory pairs: a shy fireworks show
#   view      an unusual point of view: the world seen by an ant
#   drama     tiny dramas: the last cookie standoff
#   title     famous-sounding invented titles: the phantom of the bakery
#   animal    animals with human problems: a cat filing its taxes
#   food      food with a life of its own: soup that fights back
#   place     places that should not exist: a school for baby dragons
#   gadget    inventions nobody needs: a hat that waters your plants
#   power     useless superpowers: the power to sneeze glitter
#   then      history gets mixed up: a caveman with a selfie stick
#   scene     everyday scenes gone wrong: grandpa's first video call
